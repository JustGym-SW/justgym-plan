Growth Path

When to Upgrade

Start cheap, watch these signals, upgrade when they trigger.

  • 1

    Zapier Starter → Professional ($49/mo)

    When your Zapier task count hits 600+ per month or you need more than 20 Zaps. Check the Zapier dashboard weekly in the first month. The Professional plan also unlocks branching logic which makes automations smarter.

  • 2

    Hunter Free → Starter ($34/mo)

    When the outbound campaign is running daily and producing replies or conversions. The free tier is fine for testing at 2–3 days a week. Starter gives 500 searches/month which covers a daily cap of 20 businesses comfortably.

  • 3

    ManyChat Free → Pro ($15/mo)

    When your combined Instagram and Facebook contact count approaches 1,000. The Pro plan also has built-in AI reply features that can handle simpler social interactions without calling Claude, which reduces API costs.

  • 4

    Make (Integromat) as a Zapier alternative

    If Zapier costs become a concern, Make offers 10,000 operations per month for $9. The interface is different but it handles all the same integrations. Worth switching if you are consistently on Zapier Professional and want to cut that cost significantly.
