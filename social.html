Section 04

Social Media Responses

Claude monitors Instagram and Facebook for new comments and messages, drafts natural replies in JustGym's voice, and posts them automatically. Every interaction is logged.

Platforms

What Gets Covered

Two platforms, three types of interaction — each handled slightly differently based on what the API supports.

Instagram Comments

New comments on JustGym posts are detected, analysed by Claude, and replied to directly on the post. Works for Business accounts connected via Instagram for Business.

InstagramBusinessCLIAPI

Facebook Comments

New comments on the JustGym Facebook Page are detected and replied to automatically via Facebook Pages. Claude matches the tone of the original post when drafting.

FacebookV2CLIAPI

Facebook Messages

Messages sent to the JustGym Facebook Page via Messenger are picked up and replied to by Claude. Instagram DMs require manual handling due to Meta API restrictions — see note below.

FacebookMessengerCLIAPI
Instagram DMs: Meta's API does not currently allow third-party tools to read or send Instagram Direct Messages reliably. Instagram DMs need to be handled manually or through Meta Business Suite. This may change as Meta updates its API access policies.

Voice & Tone

How Claude Should Sound

Social replies need to feel off-the-cuff, not corporate. Claude is briefed differently here than it is for email.

Comments (Instagram & Facebook)

Keep it short — one or two sentences at most.

Match the energy of the comment. A hype comment gets an enthusiastic reply. A question gets a direct answer or a nudge to DM for more detail.

Use the person's name or handle where natural. Do not over-explain. No hashtags unless they were already in the original post.

Messages (Facebook Messenger)

Slightly warmer and more conversational than comment replies.

Answer the question directly if the Knowledge Base has the answer. If not, let them know someone will follow up shortly — do not leave them hanging with a non-answer.

Sign off naturally — no formal "Kind regards" style sign-off. Just the message.

  • —Do not reveal pricing, membership rates, or package details in public comments. Direct those questions to DMs or the website.
  • —Do not respond to complaints or negative comments automatically. Flag for human review.
  • —Do not engage with spam, promotional comments, or accounts with no profile picture and zero posts.
  • —Do not mention competitors by name.
  • —Never confirm or deny membership status for any individual in a public reply.
  • —Emails from sleeper members should not be auto-replied to. Route to the human agent.
Escalation

When to Route to a Human

Not everything should be handled automatically. These cases go straight to the team.

Always Escalate

Complaints or negative sentiment
Refund or cancellation requests
Injury or health-related messages
Any message that mentions legal action
Sleeper member interactions
Anything Claude cannot confidently answer

How Escalation Works

If the interaction meets any escalation condition, the automation skips the reply step and sends a WhatsApp alert to the team with the platform, username, and message content. The interaction is logged as Escalated.
