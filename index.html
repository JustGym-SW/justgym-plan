At a Glance

Key Rules

The most important rules that apply across every automation.

Member Routing

Not all inbound emails should be auto-replied to. The automation checks the sender against the member database before processing and routes accordingly.

Client Replies

Auto-replies use Knowledge Base content only. No internal names, system details, pricing outside the KB, or any mention of AI or automation.

Outbound Limits

Maximum 20 outreach emails per day. No business contacted more than once within 90 days. No pricing or guarantees in any email copy.

Summary

How It All Fits Together

One Google Workspace account powers everything — two inboxes, two automations, one shared log.

support@justgym.co.uk

Receives all member and public enquiries. The inbound automation checks this inbox every hour, replies automatically where possible, or escalates to your human agent via WhatsApp.

outreach@justgym.co.uk

Used exclusively for the B2B campaign. The outbound automation sends from this address daily at 9 AM so outreach never touches the support inbox.
