01

Moving to Gmail

Centralise all communication into Google Workspace so every automation connects from a single account.

Why Gmail? All automations — inbound triage, outbound outreach, and logging — connect directly to Gmail via Zapier with no extra configuration. One account, one inbox, full automation.
  • 01

    Set up Google Workspace

    Sign up for Google Workspace Business Starter from £5.20 per user per month. Use your existing domain such as hello@justgym.co.uk. This provides Gmail, Sheets, and Drive — everything the automations require.

    workspace.google.com
  • 02

    Create two separate inboxes

    Set up support@justgym.co.uk for inbound member enquiries and outreach@justgym.co.uk for the B2B campaign. Keeping them separate ensures the automations never conflict with each other.

  • 03

    Migrate existing emails

    Export from your current provider as MBOX and import using the Google Workspace Migration Tool. All historical emails remain accessible. Google provides step-by-step instructions for every major email provider.

    Google Migration Tool
  • 04

    Update DNS records

    Add Google MX records at your domain registrar. Propagation takes 24 to 48 hours. Also add SPF and DMARC records at the same time to protect your sender reputation from day one.

    MX RecordsSPFDMARC
  • 05

    Connect both Gmail accounts to Zapier

    Zapier handles authentication via Google OAuth — no passwords stored. Connect both the support and outreach inboxes. All automations will be ready to activate immediately after.

    GoogleMailV2CLIAPI
  • 06

    Create the Google Sheets log

    In Google Drive, create a spreadsheet called JustGym — Automation Log with two sheets: Inbound Triage and Outbound Outreach. Connect to Zapier so both automations can write rows automatically.

    GoogleSheetsV2CLIAPI
02

Inbound Email Strategy

Runs every hour automatically — every inbound email is either answered from the Knowledge Base or escalated to a human agent.

Absolute Rule — Sleeper Members
Under no circumstances should a sleeper member be contacted, prompted, or re-engaged through any automated email. If an inbound email is identified as coming from a sleeper member, it must be escalated to a human agent immediately and not auto-replied to. The automation must never send content that could remind or encourage a sleeper member to return.

Email Reply Rules

—Replies must use Knowledge Base content only. Do not add information not present in the Knowledge Base.
—Do not include internal staff names, team names, department names, or system names.
—Do not mention AI, automation, Zapier, or any internal tooling.
—Do not include pricing, promotions, or offers of any kind unless the Knowledge Base explicitly states them.
—Always sign off as The Support Team — never with an individual name.
—Sleeper members must never receive an automated reply under any circumstances. Route to human immediately.

Outbound B2B Campaign

Runs daily at 9:00 AM — finds local businesses, sources contact emails, has Claude draft a personalised pitch, and logs every attempt.

Apollo is not required. Apollo is designed for large global B2B databases. For finding businesses near a specific location, SerpAPI (Google Maps) is more accurate, faster to set up, and significantly cheaper. It searches by radius and returns live results.

  • 01

    Find local businesses via SerpAPI

    SerpAPI queries Google Maps for businesses within your target radius. Filter by type: offices, retail, hospitality, healthcare, tech. Returns business name, address, website, phone, and category. No manual searching required.

    SerpAPIGoogle Maps
  • 02

    Find a verified contact email via Hunter.io

    Pass the business website to Hunter to locate a verified email for the decision maker. Target titles: HR Manager, Office Manager, Director, MD, Owner. If no verified email is found, skip the business and log it as No Email Found.

    Hunter.ioEmailHunterCLIAPI
  • 03

    Check for recent contact in Google Sheets

    Before sending, check the outreach log for the business name and email. Skip any business contacted in the last 90 days. This protects sender reputation and avoids annoying potential leads.

    Google Sheets
  • 04

    Claude writes the email

    Claude receives the business name, contact name, industry, and employee count. It writes a short personalised pitch under 150 words that references their specific industry or team size. No pricing, no guarantees, no template tone. Signs off as JustGym Team.

    Claude (Anthropic)AnthropicCLIAPI
  • 05

    Send from outreach inbox

    Emails are sent from outreach@justgym.co.uk, keeping the support inbox clean. Subject line is fixed: A staff perk your team will actually use — JustGym. Hard daily cap of 20 emails to stay within Gmail limits.

    GmailGoogleMailV2CLIAPI
  • 06

    Log every attempt to Google Sheets

    Every business processed is recorded regardless of outcome — sent, skipped, no email found. This builds a full picture of outreach history and prevents duplicates.

    Google Sheets

Target Profile

Local businesses within radius • 5 to 200 employees • Offices, retail, hospitality, healthcare, tech • Decision makers: HR Manager, Office Manager, MD, Director

Safeguards

Maximum 20 emails per day • Skip if contacted within 90 days • Skip businesses under 5 employees • No pricing or guarantees in any email copy
