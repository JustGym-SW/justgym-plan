Moving to Gmail

Centralise all communication into Google Workspace

Why Gmail? All automations (inbound triage, outbound outreach, logging) connect directly to Gmail via Zapier with zero extra setup. One account, one inbox, full automation.

  • 1

    Set up Google Workspace

    Sign up for Google Workspace (Business Starter from £5.20/user/month). Use your existing domain (e.g. hello@justgym.co.uk). This gives you Gmail + Sheets + Drive — everything the automations need.

    workspace.google.com
  • 2

    Set up two Gmail inboxes

    Create two addresses: support@justgym.co.uk (inbound triage automation) and outreach@justgym.co.uk (outbound B2B campaign). Keep them separate so automations don't conflict.

  • 3

    Migrate existing emails

    Use Google Workspace Migration Tool or simply forward existing emails. Export from your current provider as MBOX and import via Google Takeout. Historical emails stay accessible.

    Google Migration Tool
  • 4

    Update DNS records

    Add Google's MX records in your domain registrar. Takes 24–48hrs to propagate. Google Workspace gives you step-by-step instructions per registrar (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, etc.).

    MX RecordsSPFDMARC
  • 5

    Connect Gmail to Zapier

    In Zapier, connect both Gmail accounts (support + outreach). Zapier handles OAuth automatically — no passwords stored, uses Google's secure login. Both automations will be ready to enable.

    GoogleMailV2CLIAPI
  • 6

    Create the Google Sheets log

    In Google Drive, create a spreadsheet called JustGym — Automation Log. Add two sheets: "Inbound Triage" and "Outbound Outreach". Share it with your Google Workspace account. Connect to Zapier.

    GoogleSheetsV2CLIAPI

Outbound: B2B Staff Benefits Campaign

Daily — find local businesses, draft personalised pitches with Claude, send & track

No Apollo needed. Apollo is built for large B2B databases globally. For local businesses within a radius of your gym, SerpAPI (Google Maps) is better — it finds businesses by proximity and returns real, live results. Cheaper and more relevant.

  • 1

    Find local businesses — SerpAPI (Google Maps)

    SerpAPI queries Google Maps for businesses within your target radius. Filter by type: offices, retailers, hospitality, healthcare, tech. Returns business name, address, website, phone, category.

    SerpAPIGoogle Maps
  • 2

    Find contact email — Hunter.io

    Pass the business website to Hunter to find a verified contact email. Target titles: HR Manager, Office Manager, Director, MD, Owner. Skip the business if no verified email is found — log as "No Email Found."

    Hunter
  • 3

    Check for recent contact — Google Sheets

    Before sending, check the outreach log. Skip any business contacted in the last 90 days. Prevents duplicate outreach and keeps your sender reputation clean.

    Google Sheets
  • 4

    Claude drafts the email

    Claude receives: business name, contact name, industry, employee count. It writes a short (<150 words), human, personalised pitch — referencing their industry or team size. No pricing, no guarantees, no template feel. Signs off as "JustGym Team."

    Claude (Anthropic)
  • 5

    Send via Gmail

    Sends from outreach@justgym.co.uk — separate from the support inbox. Subject line is fixed: "A staff perk your team will actually use — JustGym." Daily cap: 20 emails max.

    Gmail
  • 6

    Log to Google Sheets

    Every attempt (sent, skipped, no email) is logged with timestamp, business details, contact info, and status.

    Google Sheets

Target Profile

Local businesses · 5–200 employees · Offices, retail, hospitality, healthcare, tech · Decision maker: HR/Office Manager, MD, Director

Safeguards

Max 20 emails/day · Skip if contacted within 90 days · Skip if under 5 employees · No pricing or guarantees in email copy
