Moving to Gmail

Centralise all communication into Google Workspace

Why Gmail? All automations (inbound triage, outbound outreach, logging) connect directly to Gmail via Zapier with no extra setup. One account, one inbox, full automation.
  • 1

    Set up Google Workspace

    Sign up for Google Workspace (Business Starter from £5.20/user/month). Use your existing domain (e.g. hello@justgym.co.uk). This gives you Gmail, Sheets, and Drive — everything the automations need.

    workspace.google.com
  • 2

    Set up two Gmail inboxes

    Create two addresses: support@justgym.co.uk for inbound triage and outreach@justgym.co.uk for the B2B campaign. Keep them separate so the automations do not conflict.

  • 3

    Migrate existing emails

    Use the Google Workspace Migration Tool or forward existing emails. Export from your current provider as MBOX and import via Google Takeout. Historical emails remain accessible.

    Google Migration Tool
  • 4

    Update DNS records

    Add Google MX records in your domain registrar. Takes 24–48 hours to propagate. Google Workspace provides step-by-step instructions per registrar (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, etc.).

    MX RecordsSPFDMARC
  • 5

    Connect Gmail to Zapier

    In Zapier, connect both Gmail accounts. Zapier handles OAuth automatically — no passwords stored, uses Google secure login. Both automations will then be ready to enable.

    GoogleMailV2CLIAPI
  • 6

    Create the Google Sheets log

    In Google Drive, create a spreadsheet called JustGym — Automation Log. Add two sheets: Inbound Triage and Outbound Outreach. Connect it to Zapier.

    GoogleSheetsV2CLIAPI

Outbound: B2B Staff Benefits Campaign

Daily at 9:00 AM — find local businesses, draft personalised pitches with Claude, send and track

Apollo is not needed. Apollo is built for large global B2B databases. For local businesses within a radius of your gym, SerpAPI (Google Maps) is better — it searches by proximity and returns live results. It is cheaper and more geographically relevant.

Log Row
  • 1

    Find local businesses — SerpAPI

    SerpAPI queries Google Maps for businesses within your target radius. Filter by type: offices, retail, hospitality, healthcare, tech. Returns business name, address, website, phone, and category.

    SerpAPIGoogle Maps
  • 2

    Find contact email — Hunter.io

    Pass the business website to Hunter to find a verified contact email. Target titles: HR Manager, Office Manager, Director, MD, Owner. Skip the business if no verified email is found and log as No Email Found.

    Hunter
  • 3

    Check for recent contact — Google Sheets

    Before sending, check the outreach log. Skip any business contacted in the last 90 days. Prevents duplicate outreach and protects sender reputation.

    Google Sheets
  • 4

    Claude drafts the email

    Claude receives business name, contact name, industry, and employee count. It writes a short (<150 words), human, personalised pitch referencing their industry or team size. No pricing, no guarantees, no template feel. Signs off as JustGym Team.

    Claude (Anthropic)
  • 5

    Send via Gmail

    Sends from outreach@justgym.co.uk — separate from the support inbox. Subject line is fixed: “A staff perk your team will actually use — JustGym.” Daily cap: 20 emails maximum.

    Gmail
  • 6

    Log to Google Sheets

    Every attempt (sent, skipped, no email found) is recorded with timestamp, business details, contact info, and status.

    Google Sheets

Target Profile

Local businesses within radius • 5–200 employees • Offices, retail, hospitality, healthcare, tech • Decision maker: HR Manager, Office Manager, MD, Director

Safeguards

Maximum 20 emails per day • Skip if contacted within 90 days • Skip businesses under 5 employees • No pricing or guarantees in email copy
